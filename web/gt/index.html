    Ground-truth editor

    Check the whole page against the scan, not only the listed disagreements. Then press Verified ✓. Full rules are in gt/CONVENTIONS.md.

    • Region markers go on their own lines: #head #body #margin #foot #pageno #other.
    • One paragraph per line, with end-of-line hyphenation removed. Spelling, æ/œ and [ ] are typed as printed.
    • {italic} · <letterspaced> · ^1 for a footnote reference · »…» for quotations · ? or [...] when the scan itself is illegible.

    Keys

    • Ctrl+S save · Ctrl+I wrap the selection in { } · Ctrl+U wrap it in < >
    • Alt+↓/Alt+↑ next/previous disagreement · Alt+PageDown/Alt+PageUp next/previous page
    • Scan: scroll the wheel to zoom and drag to pan. With follow cursor on, the word at the caret is boxed using the PDF text layer's word positions.

    Disagreements

    Your text is aligned word by word to the PDF text layer. Every place where they differ is listed with the readings of all engines. Use replaces your text with that reading. Dismiss means you have checked the place and your text is right.
